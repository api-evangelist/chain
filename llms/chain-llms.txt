# Chain

> Chain is a stablecoin payments infrastructure platform that lets enterprises move money globally across traditional banking rails and digital-currency networks. Its unified REST API covers funding, payouts and payees, virtual and physical card issuing, wallet registration with compliance screening, and a double-entry ledger. Chain connects ACH, Fedwire, FedNow, SEPA, Faster Payments, Visa and Mastercard with multi-stablecoin support (USDC, USDT, USDP, RLUSD).
